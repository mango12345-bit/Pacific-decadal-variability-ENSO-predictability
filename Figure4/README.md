# Figure 4

This directory contains the analysis code and processed input data used to reproduce **Figure 4** of the manuscript.

Figure 4 combines the historical and near-term evolution of the Pacific Decadal Oscillation (PDO), Atlantic Multidecadal Oscillation (AMO), and global warming influence (GWI) to evaluate the near-term evolution of the climate conditions examined in the manuscript.

The directory also contains an auxiliary script documenting the construction of the three future AMO scenarios used in the Figure 4 analysis.

---

---

## Analysis script

### `figure4_analysis.py`

This is the main analysis script used to reproduce **Figure 4**.

The script reads the historical PDO and AMO indices together with the future PDO prediction, future AMO scenarios, and historical and future GWI data provided in the `data/` directory.

It then performs the analyses and generates the results presented in Figure 4.

To reproduce Figure 4, the supplied processed input files can be used directly. It is not necessary to regenerate the future PDO prediction or the three future AMO scenarios before running the Figure 4 analysis.

---

## Auxiliary AMO scenario script

### `amo_three_scenarios.py`

This auxiliary script documents and reproduces the construction of the three future AMO scenarios used in the manuscript.

The script starts from the historical AMO time series and applies the three scenario-generation methods described in the Methods section of the manuscript.

The resulting future AMO trajectories used in Figure 4 are already provided in:

```text
data/AMO_future_10yr_three_methods_wide.csv
```

Running `amo_three_scenarios.py` is therefore **not required to reproduce Figure 4**, because the resulting AMO scenario data are already included in the repository.

The script is provided to document how the three future AMO scenarios were constructed and to facilitate reproduction of this part of the methodology.

---

## Input data

All input data required by `figure4_analysis.py` are provided in the `data/` directory.

### `pdo_index.1854-2025.nc`

Historical PDO index used in the Figure 4 analysis.

### `AMO_index_1854-2025.nc`

Historical AMO index used in the Figure 4 analysis.

This time series also provides the historical AMO information used to construct the three future AMO scenarios documented by `amo_three_scenarios.py`.

### `future_10yr_pdo_prediction.csv`

Near-term PDO prediction generated by the DL-PDO framework described in the manuscript.

The DL-PDO training procedure, processed training data, selected hyperparameters, ensemble metadata, and trained neural-network models are provided separately in the `DL_PDO/` directory of this repository.

This CSV file is provided directly in the Figure 4 directory as the PDO prediction input used for the published Figure 4 analysis.

### `AMO_future_10yr_three_methods_wide.csv`

Future AMO trajectories corresponding to the three AMO scenarios considered in the manuscript.

These trajectories are used directly by `figure4_analysis.py`.

The construction of the three scenarios is documented and reproducible using:

```text
amo_three_scenarios.py
```

### `global_warming_index_annual_raw.csv`

Historical annual global warming influence (GWI) time series used in the Figure 4 analysis.

### `GWI_future_10yr_recent30_quadratic_with_uncertainty.csv`

Near-term GWI trajectory and associated uncertainty used for the future portion of the Figure 4 analysis.

## Reproducing Figure 4

The processed inputs required to reproduce Figure 4 are already included in the `data/` directory.

From the `Figure4` directory, run:

```bash
python figure4_analysis.py
```

The script reads the supplied historical and future input data and generates the Figure 4 analysis and corresponding output files.

The basic reproduction workflow is:

```text
Historical PDO ───────────────┐
                              │
Historical AMO ───────────────┤
                              │
Future DL-PDO prediction ─────┤
                              │
Future AMO scenarios ─────────┼──> figure4_analysis.py ──> Figure 4
                              │
Historical GWI ───────────────┤
                              │
Future GWI ───────────────────┘
```

---

## Relationship to the DL-PDO model

The future PDO prediction used in Figure 4 is generated by the DL-PDO framework developed in the manuscript.

The complete DL-PDO training resources are provided separately in:

```text
DL_PDO/
```

That directory contains the processed climate-index time series used for model training, the DL-PDO training script, selected hyperparameters, ensemble metadata, and the 20 trained neural-network ensemble members used in the study.

For convenience and direct reproduction of Figure 4, the resulting near-term PDO prediction is also provided here as:

```text
data/future_10yr_pdo_prediction.csv
```

Users therefore do not need to retrain the DL-PDO model in order to reproduce Figure 4.

---

## Relationship to the AMO scenarios

The three future AMO trajectories used in Figure 4 are provided directly in:

```text
data/AMO_future_10yr_three_methods_wide.csv
```

The auxiliary script:

```text
amo_three_scenarios.py
```

is provided to document and reproduce how these three scenarios were constructed from the historical AMO record.

Users interested only in reproducing Figure 4 can use the supplied CSV directly.

## Data provenance

The historical climate indices and processed future trajectories supplied in the `data/` directory correspond to the data used in the Figure 4 analysis reported in the manuscript.

The future PDO prediction is derived from the DL-PDO framework described in the manuscript and documented in the `DL_PDO/` directory.

The future AMO trajectories correspond to the three scenarios considered in the manuscript, with their construction documented by `amo_three_scenarios.py`.

The historical and future GWI time series correspond to those used in the published Figure 4 analysis.

The processed data are provided directly to facilitate reproduction of the published results. The original observational and model datasets remain subject to the terms and conditions of their respective data providers.

Full descriptions of the original datasets, methodological definitions, references, and availability information are provided in the manuscript and its Data Availability statement.

## Notes

The definitions of the PDO, AMO, and GWI; the construction of the future scenarios; the DL-PDO prediction framework; and the statistical procedures used to produce Figure 4 follow those described in the Methods section of the manuscript.

This directory is intended to reproduce the published Figure 4 analysis from the supplied processed inputs while also providing the auxiliary code needed to document the construction of the future AMO scenarios.
